ProjectNyra.com Landing Design Brief

Public product marketing front door for Project Nyra. It explains the platform to brokers and partners while staying separate from the logged in command center.

Audience

  • Mortgage brokers evaluating AI-assisted automation.
  • Small teams needing lead, campaign, quote, compliance, and CRM coordination.
  • Operations partners validating audit, compliance, and assistant boundaries.

Experience Goals

  • Present a real mortgage automation product, not a generic AI demo.
  • Separate public product story from subscriber webapp and internal admin surfaces.
  • Make compliance, CRM ownership, and quote determinism part of the value prop.

Structure

  1. Hero with Project Nyra name, offer/category, preview media, and CTA.
  2. Problem statement around manual follow-up, quote drift, and compliance risk.
  3. Platform modules for lead ingestion, campaigns, compliance, quote service, command center, assistant tooling.
  4. Architecture trust band: Twenty CRM system of record, workflow engines as glue, bounded assistant.
  5. Workflow story from lead arrival through audit and broker action.
  6. Security/compliance and request-access CTA.

Do Not Include

No full subscriber controls, raw service URLs, internal hostnames, exposed infrastructure, or claims that the assistant can invent quotes or mutate CRM directly.

Success Criteria

  • A broker understands the product within one scroll.
  • Public page has no internal-only route leakage.
  • Marketing remains distinct from daily-use subscriber workflow.
