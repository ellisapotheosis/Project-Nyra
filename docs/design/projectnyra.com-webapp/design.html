ProjectNyra.com Webapp Design Brief

Subscriber and operator command center for daily broker work. It is dense, functional, and separate from the public marketing landing page.

Product Boundary

  • Twenty CRM is the system of record.
  • Nyra services own business logic.
  • n8n and Activepieces are execution glue only.
  • Assistant surfaces propose safe actions but do not mutate CRM/databases directly.
  • Quote values come from deterministic quote services.

Experience Goals

  • Fast scanning of lead urgency, compliance state, campaign progress, quote status, and CRM sync.
  • Clear live, cached, mock, degraded, and blocked labels.
  • Risky actions show approval, compliance, audit, and rollback context.

Routes

  • Home: command deck, priority queue, lead radar, service state.
  • Leads: queue, detail, timeline, conversation, compliance, audit.
  • Campaigns: operations dashboard and gated sequence builder.
  • Quotes: deterministic quote desk with assumptions and lock state.
  • Pipeline and Applications: stage, document, milestone, and blocker workflows.
  • CRM: Twenty-backed overview and sync state.
  • Assistant/OpenClaw: bounded proposed actions.
  • Admin/Integrations/Settings: provider health, required secrets, owner actions.

Component Rules

Use shadcn tokens, compact icons, badges, tabs, skeletons, stable list heights, disabled action reasons, and no marketing hero sections after login.

Success Criteria

  • Daily broker workflow can be completed inside the webapp except for protected external CRM/tool links.
  • Compliance blockers are impossible to miss.
  • Quote, campaign, assistant, and CRM boundaries are visible.
