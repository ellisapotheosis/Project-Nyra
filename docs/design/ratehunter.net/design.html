RateHunter.net Design Brief

Public borrower-facing marketing and lead capture for Ellis Andersen: fast, trustworthy, personal, and conversion oriented without internal Project Nyra exposure.

Audience

  • Borrowers comparing purchase, refinance, HELOC, and specialty mortgage options.
  • Referral partners who need booking, contact, or intake links.
  • Existing borrowers returning for secure upload, application, or chat support.

Experience Goals

  • First viewport makes the broker, RateHunter brand, and primary actions unmistakable.
  • Lead capture is visible without making the page feel like a generic form.
  • Borrowers can schedule, start intake, apply, request HELOC help, upload docs, call, text, or email.
  • Compliance copy stays educational and non-binding.

Visual Direction

Apotheosis dark mortgage concierge: near-black background, indigo primary actions, seafoam support states, neon pink only for meaningful alerts, and glass panels over real broker assets.

Page Structure

  1. Sticky compact nav.
  2. Hero with portrait, contact card, roles, and CTAs.
  3. Action grid for consult, application, HELOC, upload, company, and contact card.
  4. Borrower quote intake wizard.
  5. Service lines and trust proof.
  6. Market pulse with educational disclaimer.
  7. Contact footer and bounded borrower chat.

Do Not Include

No CRM, MCP, OpenClaw, Nexus, Twenty, n8n, Activepieces, Grafana, Portainer, worker links, subscriber dashboard controls, or internal route names.

Success Criteria

  • Borrower understands who Ellis is and can start the right action in under 10 seconds.
  • Public source scan finds no internal admin/tool links.
  • Mobile form and chat remain usable without overlap.
