# CRM API Contract

`services/crm-api` is the only app-facing boundary for Twenty CRM writes and custom object access.

## Responsibilities

- Normalize app and ingestion payloads into Twenty CRM records.
- Own Twenty GraphQL/REST mapping and custom object field names.
- Upsert contacts, mortgage leads, loans, campaign enrollment mirrors, communication logs, quote mirrors, tasks, and notes.
- Emit append-only audit and timeline events for durable mutations.

## Forbidden

- No quote calculation.
- No campaign scheduling/state-machine ownership.
- No communication provider dispatch.
- No assistant-direct CRM mutation outside proposed action approval and audit.
- No direct CRM database access from apps or agents.

## Frontend Calls

- `GET /api/leads`
- `POST /api/leads`
- `GET /api/leads/:id`
- `PATCH /api/leads/:id/status`
- `GET /api/leads/:id/timeline`
- `GET /api/leads/:id/conversation`
- `POST /api/leads/:id/tasks`
- `POST /api/leads/:id/notes`
- `POST /api/leads/:id/quote`
- `POST /api/leads/:id/quote-mirrors`
- `POST /api/leads/:id/communication-logs`
- `POST /api/leads/:id/campaigns/enroll`
- `POST /api/leads/:id/campaigns/pause`
- `POST /api/webhooks/reply`

All request and response bodies should use `@nyra/domain-models` schemas.

## Campaign and Reply Rules

- `POST /api/leads/:id/campaigns/enroll` creates a local campaign enrollment mirror and marks the Twenty mortgage lead campaign status `ACTIVE`.
- `POST /api/leads/:id/campaigns/pause` pauses active or pending local enrollment mirrors and marks the Twenty mortgage lead campaign status `PAUSED`.
- `POST /api/webhooks/reply` logs inbound reply metadata when local lead metadata exists, pauses campaign enrollment, and marks the Twenty mortgage lead `PAUSED`.
- STOP, unsubscribe, remove, cancel, opt-out, or DNC intent in reply text marks the Twenty mortgage lead `STOPPED` and records a STOP audit event.

## Required Prechecks

- Borrower communication log writes must include a `ComplianceDecision`.
- Quote mirror writes must reference a quote generated by `services/quote-api`.
- Campaign enrollment mirror writes must reference a campaign state owned by `services/campaign-engine`.

## Audit Evidence

Every mutation records `correlationId`, `idempotencyKey`, actor, source service, timestamp, and related CRM record IDs.
