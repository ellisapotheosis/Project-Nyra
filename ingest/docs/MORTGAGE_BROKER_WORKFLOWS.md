## Loan Lifecycle Workflows

### Stage 1: Lead Inquiry → Pre-Approval (24-72 hours)

**Steps**:
1. Lead submits inquiry via RateHunter.net
2. Nyra qualifies lead (10 min)
3. Ellis calls lead within 2 hours (hot leads)
4. Schedule Zoom consultation (15-30 min)
5. Collect verbal application info
6. Run soft credit check
7. Provide verbal pre-approval
8. Email pre-approval letter PDF
9. Enroll in "Pre-Approval" drip campaign

**Tools**: Nyra AI, CRM, LOS (credit pull), Zoom, DocuSign

---

### Stage 2: Pre-Approval → Full Application (3-7 days)

**Steps**:
1. Send document checklist (via Nyra automation)
2. Borrower uploads docs to secure portal
3. Nyra OCR processes documents
4. Ellis reviews for completeness
5. Submit full application to LOS
6. Pay for full credit report
7. Order appraisal (if purchase contract received)
8. Send Loan Estimate within 3 business days (TILA requirement)

**Tools**: LOS, document portal, OCR engine, appraisal management company

---

### Stage 3: Application → Underwriting (7-14 days)

**Steps**:
1. Processor reviews file for completeness
2. Order title, flood cert, VOE (verification of employment)
3. Submit to underwriter
4. Underwriter issues conditional approval
5. Ellis works with borrower to clear conditions
6. Re-submit to underwriter
7. Receive Clear to Close

**Tools**: LOS, underwriting platform, e-signature (DocuSign)

---

### Stage 4: Clear to Close → Funding (3-5 days)

**Steps**:
1. Schedule closing with title company
2. Review Closing Disclosure with borrower (3-day waiting period)
3. Conduct final walkthrough (if purchase)
4. Borrower signs closing docs at title
5. Lender wires funds to escrow
6. Loan records with county
7. Celebrate! 🎉

**Tools**: Title/escrow software, wire confirmation, county recorder

---

### Stage 5: Post-Close (Ongoing)

**Steps**:
1. Send thank-you gift (Amazon gift card, branded swag)
2. Request Google Review
3. Add to "Past Clients" drip campaign
4. Monitor for refinance opportunities
5. Request referrals at 6-month mark

**Tools**: CRM, Nyra automation, email marketing

## Compliance Workflows

### TILA Disclosure Timing
- **Loan Estimate**: Within 3 business days of application
- **Closing Disclosure**: At least 3 business days before closing
- **APR Tolerance**: Must be within 0.125% of final APR

**Workflow**:
1. LOS auto-generates Loan Estimate
2. Ellis reviews for accuracy
3. Email to borrower via secure link
4. Track receipt confirmation
5. Store in loan file

---

### HMDA Data Collection
**Required Fields**:
- Ethnicity
- Race
- Sex
- Age (or year of birth)
- Income

**Workflow**:
1. Borrower completes demographic questionnaire (optional disclosure)
2. Store in LOS
3. Submit to CFPB annually via LOS
4. Analyze for fair lending compliance

---

### Adverse Action Notices
**Triggers**:
- Denial
- Counteroffer (higher rate than quoted)
- Conditional approval (not full approval)

**Workflow**:
1. LOS flags adverse action
2. Ellis reviews denial reasons
3. Auto-generate adverse action notice (via Nyra)
4. Mail/email within 30 days
5. Include credit bureau contact info
6. Store in compliance folder

## Emergency Procedures

### Client Urgency (Rate Lock Expiring Today)
1. Call client immediately
2. Check current market rates vs locked rate
3. Extend rate lock if better for client (pay extension fee)
4. Or float to close if rates improved
5. Document decision in loan file

### Underwriting Denial 24 Hours Before Closing
1. Call client with transparency
2. Identify denial reason
3. Explore alternative lenders (if applicable)
4. Request contract extension from seller
5. Work to resolve issue (additional docs, explanation letters)
6. Re-submit to underwriter

### System Downtime (LOS Offline)
1. Switch to manual processing (spreadsheets, PDF forms)
2. Contact LOS support for ETA
3. Communicate delays to clients
4. Catch up on data entry when system restored
