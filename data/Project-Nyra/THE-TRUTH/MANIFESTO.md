# Manifesto: The Truth of Project Nyra

## 1. Mission

Project Nyra is a decentralized, local-first AI orchestration platform. It is built to serve as a seamless, powerful, and private extension of its operator's creative and developmental will. It transforms a heterogeneous network of personal computing devices into a cohesive, intelligent system that is greater than the sum of its parts.

Our mission is to **unify local and cloud AI capabilities** under a single, flexible, and resilient orchestration layer, ensuring that the operator retains full control, ownership, and privacy.

## 2. Core Principles

*   **Local-First, Cloud-Assisted:** The center of gravity is the local network. All core orchestration, memory, and agentic workflows run on hardware you control. Cloud services are treated as powerful, but optional, extensions—leveraged for scale and specialized capabilities, not as a dependency for core function.
*   **Interoperability by Design:** The system is built on the principle of open standards and protocols. We use MetaMCP as a universal translator, allowing heterogeneous agents, tools, and services (Claude, Gemini, Archon, local LLMs) to communicate and collaborate without bespoke integrations. We embrace A2A and ANP as forward-looking standards for a truly open agent ecosystem.
*   **Resilience and Decentralization:** The network is a mesh, not a monarchy. Using Tailscale, every node is a first-class citizen. Orchestration and tool-providing capabilities can be distributed. The failure of one node should not bring down the entire system.
*   **Declarative & Reproducible:** The entire stack is defined as code. From the `docker-compose.yml` that bootstraps the services to the `channels.json` that defines the agentic nervous system, the setup is version-controlled, auditable, and rapidly reproducible on any machine.
*   **Developer-Centric:** The primary user is a developer. The system is designed to accelerate development workflows. Claude-Flow provides repo-aware orchestration, A2A gateways enable complex multi-agent systems, and the entire stack is observable and debuggable.

## 3. The Vision

Project Nyra will become a living, evolving partner in creation. It will manage its own infrastructure, learn from its interactions, and proactively assist in the development of new software, ideas, and workflows. It is the foundation for a personal AI super-intelligence, built not in a distant cloud, but right here at home.

This document, and this entire `THE-TRUTH` directory, serves as the single source of truth for this vision. It is a living document, evolving with the project itself.
