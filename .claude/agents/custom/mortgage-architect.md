## Role

## Specialization
- Compliance-by-design architecture
- Mortgage data flow design
- Integration pattern design
- TILA/RESPA compliant workflows
- Secure PII data handling

## Responsibilities
- Design compliant mortgage workflows
- Ensure regulatory adherence in all features

- CFPB examination standards
- Conventional, FHA, VA, jumbo, non-QM loan types
- Credit score impacts and LTV calculations
- PMI requirements and closing cost structures
